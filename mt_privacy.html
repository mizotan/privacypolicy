POLITIKA TAL-PRIVATEZZA (Malti)

Bonsai Project / 盆栽企画 ("aħna") jistabbilixxi din il-Politika tal-Privatezza dwar l-immaniġġjar tal-informazzjoni tal-utent fl-applikazzjonijiet kollha ("Apps") żviluppati u pprovduti minna. (Għal-lista speċifika tal-applikazzjonijiet koperti, jekk jogħġbok irreferi għall-paġna ewlenija).

1. Ġbir ta' Informazzjoni u Għan

  • Identifikaturi tal-Apparat (Apps bir-Reklami): Fl-Apps li juru r-reklami, is-servizzi ta' reklamar ta' partijiet terzi (bħal Google AdMob) jistgħu jiġbru awtomatikament IDs tar-reklamar (eż., AAID) biex iwasslu reklami personalizzati u jkejlu l-prestazzjoni. Din id-dejta ma tidentifikakx personalment.
  • Permessi tal-Kamera u tal-Mikrofonu (Apps Applikabbli Biss): Fl-Apps li jinkludu funzjonijiet tal-kamera jew tal-mikrofonu, dawn il-permessi jintużaw esklussivament għall-analiżi tal-vidjo fl-app, effetti speċjali u reġistrazzjoni. L-Apps li ma jeħtiġux dawn il-funzjonijiet m'għandhomx aċċess għall-kamera jew għall-mikrofonu tiegħek. Id-dejta kollha tiġi pproċessata lokalment fuq l-apparat tiegħek u qatt ma tittella' fuq is-servers tagħna.

2. Kondiviżjoni, Kriptaġġ u Tħassir

  • Kondiviżjoni ma' Partijiet Terzi: Aħna ma naqsmux dejta personali ma' partijiet terzi, ħlief kif meħtieġ mil-liġi.
  • Sigurtà tad-Dejta: Id-dejta trażmessa permezz ta' SDKs tar-reklamar tiġi kriptata waqt it-trażmissjoni bl-użu ta' protokolli siguri (HTTPS).
  • Tħassir tad-Dejta: L-Apps tagħna ma jeħtiġux reġistrazzjoni ta' kont u ma jaħżnu l-ebda dejta tal-utent fuq is-servers tagħna. Il-fajls tal-midja jew id-dejta tal-app fuq l-apparat jistgħu jiġu ġestiti u mħassra mill-utent fi kwalunkwe ħin.

3. Ċaħda ta' Responsabbiltà

Aħna ma nassumu l-ebda responsabbiltà għal kwalunkwe ħsara jew żvantaġġ li jista' jinqala' mill-użu tal-Apps tagħna. L-Apps kollha huma pprovduti esklussivament għal skopijiet ta' divertiment.

5. Analitika tal-Użu u Ġestjoni tal-Ħlasijiet (Għal Apps Applikabbli Biss)

F'Apps li juru riklami, l-SDK tal-fornitur terz tagħna tas-servizzi tar-riklami, Google LLC (Google AdMob), jista' jiġbor awtomatikament identifikaturi tar-riklami (pereżempju, AAID) u informazzjoni dwar l-apparat biex iwassal riklami personalizzati u jkejjel l-effettività tagħhom. Barra minn hekk, sabiex intejbu l-kwalità tal-app, nirranġaw il-bugs, u nwettqu analiżi statistika tal-użu, nistgħu niġbru z-zkuk (logs) tal-operat tal-app u data dwar il-ħbit (crash data) permezz ta' moduli ta' ġbir ta' data pprovduti minn Google (bħal Firebase). Din l-informazzjoni mhix marbuta ma' informazzjoni li tidentifikak personalment b'mod dirett, bħal ismek jew id-dettalji ta' kuntatt tiegħek, u tintuża esklussivament biex jintwerew ir-riklami kif ukoll biex titkejjel u tittejjeb il-prestazzjoni tal-app.

Tista' tiddiżattiva r-riklami personalizzati permezz tas-settings tal-apparat tiegħek (Settings > Google > Ads). Jekk tinstab setting ta' protezzjoni tal-privatezza pprovduta mill-apparat jew mill-pjattaforma tiegħek (bħas-setting "Do Not Track"), aħna nirrispettaw dik is-setting u nillimitaw il-ġbir tad-data kif xieraq. Għal aktar dettalji, jekk jogħġbok aqra l-Politika tal-Privatezza ta' Google.

Jekk noffru xiri fl-app jew karatteristiċi simili fil-futur, nistgħu nipproċessaw informazzjoni dwar l-istat tal-ħlas permezz tas-sistema tal-kontijiet ta' Google Play għall-iskop li nivverifikaw l-istorja tax-xiri u l-liċenzji. Aħna ma niksbux jew naħżnu d-dettalji tal-karta tal-kreditu jew tal-kont bankarju tiegħek b'mod dirett.

6. Ipproċessar ta' Data f'Karatteristiċi mħaddma mill-Intelliġenza Artifiċjali (AI) (Għal Apps Applikabbli Biss)

F'Apps li jinkludu karatteristiċi li jużaw it-tagħlim tal-magni (machine learning) jew teknoloġiji tal-AI bħal MediaPipe, nistgħu nanalizzaw u nipproċessaw data tal-input, bħal immaġnijiet tal-kamera, vidjow, awdjo, u test, fuq l-apparat tiegħek sal-limitu meħtieġ biex tiġi pprovduta l-karatteristika.

Għal karatteristiċi li huma pproċessati kompletament fuq l-apparat tiegħek, l-immaġnijiet tal-input, il-vidjow, l-awdjo u r-riżultati tal-analiżi ma jintbagħtux lill-servers tagħna. Madankollu, skont l-ispeċifikazzjonijiet tal-SDKs jew tas-servizzi esterni użati, informazzjoni dwar l-apparat, informazzjoni dwar l-app, statistika tal-użu, informazzjoni dijanjostika, u data simili jistgħu jintbagħtu lill-fornitur tas-servizz għal skopijiet bħall-provvediment tal-karatteristika, l-aġġornament tal-mudelli, il-kejl tal-prestazzjoni, u l-analiżi tal-bugs.

Jekk nipprovdu karatteristika li tuża servizz tal-AI ibbażat fuq il-cloud jew simili u tipproċessa d-data kollha tal-input jew parti minnha fuq server estern, aħna nipprovdu l-informazzjoni meħtieġa minn qabel, bħad-data li trid tintbagħat, ir-riċevitur, u l-iskop tal-użu, permezz ta' spjegazzjonijiet fl-app jew f'din il-Politika.

Il-kamp ta' applikazzjoni attwali tal-ipproċessar tad-data ivarja skont il-karatteristiċi, it-teknoloġiji, u s-settings ta' kull App.

PRIVACY POLICY (English)

Bonsai Project / 盆栽企画 ("we") sets forth this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of user information across all applications ("Apps") developed and provided by us. (For the specific list of target applications, please refer to the main page).

1. Information Collection and Purpose

  • Device Identifiers (Apps with Ads): Third-party ad services (such as Google AdMob) may automatically collect advertising IDs (e.g., AAID) to deliver personalized ads and measure performance. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Camera and Microphone Permissions (Applicable Apps Only): In Apps that include camera or microphone features, permissions are used solely for in-app video analysis, special effects, and recording. Apps that do not require these features do not access your camera or microphone. All data is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

2. Sharing, Encryption, and Deletion

  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not share personal data with third parties except as required by law.
  • Data Security: Data transmitted via ad SDKs is encrypted in transit using secure protocols (HTTPS).
  • Data Deletion: Our Apps do not require account registration and store no user data on our servers. On-device media files or app data can be managed and deleted by the user at any time.

3. Disclaimer

We assume no liability for any damages or disadvantages arising from the use of our Apps. All Apps are provided strictly for entertainment purposes.

4. Contact

For inquiries, please contact: bonsai.project.2026@gmail.com

The following provisions supplement the sections above and apply only to Apps that include the relevant features.

5. Usage Analytics and Payment Management (Applicable Apps Only)

In Apps that display ads, the SDK of our third-party ad service provider, Google LLC (Google AdMob), may automatically collect advertising identifiers (e.g., AAID) and device information. In addition, to improve app quality, fix bugs, and perform statistical analysis of usage, we may collect app operation logs and crash data through data collection modules provided by Google (such as Firebase). This information is not linked to information that directly identifies you, such as your name or contact details, and is used solely to display ads and to measure and improve app performance.

You can disable personalized ads through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). If a privacy protection setting provided by your device or platform (such as a do-not-track setting) is detected, we will respect that setting and restrict data collection as appropriate. For details, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy.

If we offer in-app purchases or similar features in the future, we may process payment status information through the Google Play billing system for the purpose of verifying purchase history and licenses. We do not directly obtain or store your credit card or bank account information.

6. Data Processing in AI-Powered Features (Applicable Apps Only)

In Apps that include features using machine learning or AI technologies such as MediaPipe, we may analyze and process input data, such as camera images, video, audio, and text, on your device to the extent necessary to provide the feature.

For features that are processed entirely on your device, the input images, video, audio, and the analysis results are not sent to our servers. However, depending on the specifications of the SDKs or external services used, device information, app information, usage statistics, diagnostic information, and similar data may be sent to the service provider for purposes such as providing the feature, updating models, measuring performance, and analyzing bugs.

If we provide a feature that uses a cloud-based AI service or similar and processes all or part of the input data on an external server, we will provide the necessary information in advance, such as the data to be sent, the recipient, and the purpose of use, through in-app explanations or this Policy.

The actual scope of data processing varies depending on the features, technologies, and settings of each App.

7. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom

For users residing in the EEA and the United Kingdom, we manage consent for ad delivery and data processing in accordance with applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR), including through the use of the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK or similar tools. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time through the app or your device settings.
